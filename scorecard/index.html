Options: held to expiry

Each option callout is scored as if held until expiry: intrinsic value at the expiry close of the underlying minus the entry premium the channel quoted. A win means intrinsic value finished above the quoted entry. Channels usually post an early exit instead, so this is a stress test of the callouts, not anyone's account record.

Options methodology and assumptions

    Forex, gold and index signals: TP1 first, SL first

    Signals with an entry, a stop and at least one target (for example a gold sell with a zone, SL and TP1 to TP3) are scored on 5-minute price bars after the post. TP1 first means the first target traded before the stop within 24 hours of the fill; SL first means the stop traded first. Results are in R, where 1R is the distance from the fill to the stop.

    FX methodology and assumptions

      Content timeline: forex and gold versus stock and option posts

      Some channels post forex, gold or index signals and stock or option callouts at different times. This shows, per channel, the date range of each kind among posts the parser recognised. Posts whose exact timestamp is shared by 6 or more posts in one channel are bulk dumps (a history replay or backfill): their original posting dates are unknown, so they are counted separately and not used to date a switch.
