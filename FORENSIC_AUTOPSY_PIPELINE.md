# 🧬 Artemis Trader Forensic Autopsy Pipeline

This document details the standardized methodology, prompt architecture, and quantitative extraction scripts used to forensic-audit every trader in the AfterHour network and generate the quantitative dossiers in `reports/dossiers/`.

---

## 📋 The Master Subagent Prompt Template

Every single trader dossier is generated by spinning up an isolated `flash` subagent with this standardized prompt:

```text
You are a senior quantitative trading analyst conducting a deep forensic autopsy on AfterHour trader @{username} (#{rank} most active followed trader with {lifetime_posts} lifetime posts).

His complete lifetime history of {lifetime_posts} posts has been downloaded and formatted into:
- Markdown Archive: /home/mpha/artemis/afterhour/reports/posts/@{username}_lifetime.md
- Raw JSON: /home/mpha/artemis/afterhour/data/following/@{username}_all_posts.json

Read through his lifetime posts and conduct a quantitative, mechanical, and psychological breakdown:
1. Executive Profile: Trader philosophy, post cadence (posts/week), active timeline, verified capital trajectory and drawdown history.
2. Ticker Universe & Catalysts: Core assets traded, options vs equities, macro vs technical setups. What drives his trade entries?
3. Risk Management & PnL Reality: Does he take profits or hold bags? How does he manage losing positions? What are his documented wins vs blow-ups?
4. Behavioral & Sentiment Signals: Why is he posting? Decode his recurring linguistic patterns, sentiment calls, and how he reacts to market volatility/red days.
5. Quantitative Verdict for TraderMatrix:
   - Primary and Secondary Algorithmic Classification Tags (e.g. `MOMENTUM_SCALPER`, `CONTRARIAN_FADE`, etc.)
   - Algorithmic Alpha Score (0 to 100) & Expectancy
   - Signal Flow Ingestion Matrix (ASCII/Markdown table)
   - Specific Execution Directives for the Artemis Engine:
     * Ingestion Directives (what genuine edge to harvest)
     * Contrarian Fade Directives (when to fade his calls or retail herd)
     * Mandatory Risk Blacklists & Firewalls (toxic instruments, bad habits to filter out)
     * Exit Rules & Alpha Rectification (profit-taking, trailing stop rules)
6. Chronological Milestone & Catalyst Log.

Write your comprehensive, highly structured markdown report directly to:
/home/mpha/artemis/afterhour/reports/dossiers/@{username}_quant_profile.md

When finished, provide a sharp, concise summary of the key findings and verdict.
```

## 🛠️ The Quantitative Calculation Steps (How Numbers Are Computed)

Before writing a single word of prose, the agent executes Python scripts on the trader's raw JSON archive (`@{username}_all_posts.json`):

### 1. Capital Curve & Drawdown Analysis
- Reads all posts where `amount_k` is present.
- Extracts baseline capital, peak account value, trough account value, and unlinked/withdrawal events.
- Flags broker valuation anomalies (e.g., weekend mid-price illiquid options spikes).

### 2. Ticker Universe & Cashtag Extraction
- Merges the platform `tickers` metadata field with regular expressions `\$([A-Z]{1,6})\b` across post titles and bodies.
- Filters out false positives (`$USD`, `$CEO`, `$ATH`, `$DTE`, `$FOMO`).
- Ranks top 30 assets by lifetime frequency and separates them into core vs speculative peripheral baskets.

### 3. Posting Velocity & Temporal Distribution
- Calculates posts/week and posts/day across calendar regimes.
- Computes Day of Week percentages (e.g. Terri's 25.8% Sunday preparation bias).
- Computes Hourly UTC-to-EDT distribution (identifies pre-market prep vs opening bell scalps vs late-night research).

### 4. Trade Receipts & Loss Auditing
- Filters posts by tags `[Gain]`, `[Loss]`, `[Dd]`, `[Yolo]`.
- Cross-references claimed percentages against Plaid account delta changes to identify fake claims or paper-trading anomalies.
- Identifies specific exit behaviors: trailing stops, scaling out, averaging down, or bag-holding to zero.

### 5. Linguistic & Behavioral Signals
- Analyzes signature phrases, victory laps, frustration spikes during red tape, and audience monetization funnels (Substack, Discords, courses).

## 🚀 How to Run the Pipeline Manually from Terminal

To manually download and generate a dossier for any user from the command line:

```bash
# 1. Download 100% lifetime posts to JSON & Markdown:
python3 /home/mpha/artemis/afterhour/download_single_trader_lifetime.py <username>

# 2. Output files will be created at:
#    /home/mpha/artemis/afterhour/data/following/@<username>_all_posts.json
```
