### Post #8: 2026-01-20 00:47 `[Dd]`

**LPPLS**

Follow up from previous post, testing LPPLS as an oscillator, and as a strategy. It's a pretty simple buy / sell strategy that definitely needs some work but shows how it can at least be profitable.

If you look closely and long enough, you start to get a feel for which way a quake will break based on the last risk indicator. Think of the triangles loosely as buy / sell indicators but it's not quite that straightforward.

I think it really only makes sense as a highly leveraged bet around the "earthquakes" themselves, with the oscillator potentially providing a clue as to which way the earthquake would pan out. So not a super long term strategy where you just start with $10k and win over hundreds of trades (won't beat the market), but a once a quarter type trade that generates an outsized return (up or down, doesn't matter, leverage and go for it calls / puts).

It's at least captivating.

*Link: https://afterhour.com/moloch/bfza/lppls*
