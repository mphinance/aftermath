### 2026-05-16 20:46 — `$HOOD`

**With Great Leverage Comes Great Responsibility**

There have been many posts against margin and I agree with them all. They're all stating that it is not recommended to carry a margin balance. I wholeheartedly agree with this. Although there is nothing wrong if a trader wants to carry a margin balance. I just believe less debt is best.

I don't plan to get into the full details of margin accounts in this post. If users want that, I'd be happy to go into the details in a future post!

As I've said before, having a margin account and carrying a margin balance are two different things.

Just because one has a margin account does not mean they carry a margin balance. The key is to NOT spend what you DON'T have.

Personally, I have a Portfolio Margin account as my main trading account. This is riskier, but can be a great way to use leverage responsibly. One gets way more Buying Power with a Portfolio Margin account. Not all brokers offer Portfolio Margin though. For example, $HOOD only offers Regular T-Margin.

Why am I telling you this? Well, for one, you may not have known that there are two types of margin accounts (Portfolio Margin and T-Margin). Two, to have some educational material on margin accounts and how to use them responsibly. And three, to let you know that I have and use a margin account, but I rarely carry a margin balance.

Let's think of this like a credit card. If you only spend what you can afford. Your credit card is basically a debit card with delayed payment and rewards. You make purchases, pay off the balance at the due date, and never pay interest.

With margin, it's similar. We borrow against our assets for leverage. If we never spend over our cash balance, we never pay interest.

So, if we sell options, Buying Power is tied up as collateral. However, that collateral NEVER carries interest UNLESS you are assigned and don't have enough cash to cover said purchases. So when used responsibly, you can gain more leverage to grow your account.

All of this being said. If you don't have discipline, easily spend what you don't have, and/or can't scale Buying Power Usage systematically - REMOVE THE TEMPTATION. A margin account may not be for you.

Anyways, thanks for reading. I just want to be the guy discussing using a margin account in a smart and strategic manner. Education is key!
