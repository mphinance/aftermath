**Why the Market Keeps Rising (Even When Your Stocks Don't)**

Ever notice how the market keeps grinding higher even when your favorite stocks are pulling back?

There's a hidden pattern most traders miss. It's called "The Great Mini Rotation" and it's been the engine behind this bull market's resilience.

Here's how it works:

AI and tech stocks blast higher for weeks or months. Then they pause or pull back as traders take profits. But instead of the whole market tanking, something interesting happens.

Money quietly rotates into the beaten-down sectors. Healthcare and biotech start breaking out. Energy stocks begin their run. The laggards become the leaders.

Then those new leaders get extended and pause. And guess what? Money rotates back into the original leaders or finds new undervalued areas. Rinse and repeat.

This is why the indexes can feel choppy day-to-day but still work their way to new highs. It's not one big wave lifting everything. It's a series of smaller waves taking turns.

The contrast with bear markets is striking. In bear markets, everything falls together. There's no rotation, only liquidation. But right now? Different sectors are taking turns driving the bus.

Think about 2025. AI stocks led early in the year. Then they cooled off and healthcare/biotech took over. Now we're seeing energy stocks break out as AI consolidates. The baton keeps passing.

This pattern has played out in every major bull market since the 1990s. Small, sequential leadership rotations keeping things dynamic and self-sustaining.

The biggest risk? Complacency. Assuming this rotation will always save the market. When a real macro shock hits, all sectors can fall together. But until then, this mini rotation is keeping the bull alive.

So what does this mean for your portfolio?

Stay diversified across sectors. Watch for improving relative strength in laggards. Don't chase every rotation, but be ready to lean into areas showing fresh momentum. The key is being in harmony with the market's rhythm.

The market isn't one organism anymore. It's an ecosystem where different species thrive at different times.
