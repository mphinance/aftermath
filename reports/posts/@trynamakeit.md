### 2026-09-01 21:27

**PSA**

Receipts don’t mean shit by themselves. 

A screenshot of a green P&L doesn’t prove you’re a good trader. Screenshots can be fabricated, cherry picked, or completely devoid of context.

Someone can make $10,000 by taking absolutely horrible trades, overleveraging, ignoring risk management, and getting bailed out by luck. That doesn’t make the decisions good it just means the outcome happened to be favorable.

And that’s the biggest problem with judging traders strictly by P&L:

You cannot teach someone how to trade by showing them a P&L.

A P&L tells you what happened. It doesn’t tell you:

* Why the entry was taken
* Why the exit was taken
* What the risk was
* What the invalidation was
* Whether the setup was actually repeatable
* Whether the trader followed a process
* Whether luck played a major role

Give me a trader with consistent execution, defined risk, repeatable setups, and disciplined decision-making over someone posting a massive green screenshot any day.

Profit is the result. The process is the skill.

If your entire argument is “look at my P&L,” you haven’t proven that you’re a skilled trader.

You’ve only proven that you made money that time.
