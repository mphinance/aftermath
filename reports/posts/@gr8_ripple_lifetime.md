📊 Company Due Diligence (DD) Template

1. Basic Info
	•	Company Name & Ticker:
	•	Sector/Industry:
	•	Market Cap:
	•	Current Price / 52-Week Range:
	•	Headquarters & Operations Location(s):
	•	Primary Products/Services:


2. Investment Thesis (Why Consider This?)
	•	What problem are they solving?
	•	Unique value proposition / moat.
	•	Why now? (macro trends, sector growth, timing).

3. Business Model
	•	Revenue streams (product sales, subscriptions, licensing, etc.).
	•	Customers/clients (retail, enterprise, government).
	•	Geographic reach (domestic vs international).
	•	Partnerships or major contracts.

4. Financials

(use last 2–4 quarters or TTM)
	•	Revenue growth (YoY %):
	•	Profitability (Net Income, EPS):
	•	Margins (Gross, Operating):
	•	Cash Flow (positive/negative):
	•	Debt Load (Debt-to-Equity ratio):
	•	Cash on hand vs burn rate:

5. Valuation
	•	P/E ratio (if profitable):
	•	P/S ratio (if early stage/growth):
	•	EV/EBITDA or other key multiples vs sector averages.
	•	Compare valuation to competitors.

6. Growth Catalysts
	•	Sector tailwinds (AI, energy transition, biotech, etc.).
	•	Upcoming product launches or expansion.
	•	Government contracts or policy benefits.
	•	Strategic acquisitions/partnerships.

7. Risks
	•	Financial risks (cash burn, dilution risk, debt).
	•	Competitive risks (larger players, low barriers to entry).
	•	Regulatory risks (FDA, FCC, SEC, international laws).
	•	Market risks (commodity prices, interest rates, sector cycles).

8. Management & Ownership
	•	CEO & leadership track record.
	•	Insider ownership % (skin in the game).
	•	Institutional ownership % (hedge funds, ETFs).
	•	Recent insider buys/sells.

9. Momentum & Market Sentiment
	•	Analyst ratings (buy/hold/sell consensus).
	•	Short interest % (high = possible squeeze or bearish outlook).
	•	Recent news, hype, or controversy.
	•	Social media / retail investor sentiment.

10. Technical Analysis (Optional)
	•	Current trend (bullish, bearish, consolidation).
	•	Key support & resistance levels.
	•	Moving averages (50MA, 200MA).
	•	RSI/MACD signals.

11. Exit Strategy / Investment Horizon
	•	Target price range.
	•	Timeframe (short swing, mid-term catalyst, long-term hold).
	•	Stop-loss level (risk management).
	•	Position sizing plan (how much of portfolio).

12. Final Rating
	•	✅ Bullish Case Summary
	•	❌ Bearish Case Summary
	•	📈 Decision: Strong Buy / Buy / Neutral / Avoid / Sell
