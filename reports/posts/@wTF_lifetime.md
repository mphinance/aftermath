### Post #242: 2024-03-24 17:51

**A (challenging) week in review 🫥**

So we started the week down over $12M on $BA, Friday morning up almost $3M, and ended the week pretty much even.
 
Through out the week, I recycled almost 40% of my position (buying in more at lows and selling at highs). Fun fact: I would have recycled almost 80% if my HomePod alarm had gone off on Friday. I woke up to find it unplugged, Ronny is the only explanation that I can think of.

We were by every definition early in $BA (what a difference a few days make), but that’s ok because we won’t always get timing perfect ($SQ was a good example of that) but the mistake I made was starting off with a big position as if I had already gotten the timing right, which limited Martin’s ability to help when the stock further plummeted. If it were not for that, this would have been a much more profitable play already. But it was important to acknowledge that rather than pretending otherwise and letting Martin do his thing.

I wish I could say that was the only mistake I made. Earlier in the week my $ADBE position hit a 50%+ gain of roughly $2.5M (my goal was 30%), but instead of recycling it, I held. Why? Because $BA was down. I *hoped* that $ADBE could go higher and make up for $BA. I let my emotions get the better of me again, letting one position cloud my judgment in another.

I don’t like having these big positions, regardless of the outcome. The process takes away from my time at school and with family & friends, adding a layer (really a cloud) of preoccupation that chips away from my day-to-day enjoyment of all the things I love.

While this might sound obvious, it is a much bigger understatement that you might think: Getting into positions are easy, getting out of them is hard.

Ultimately, I go into next week with my biggest position into $BA yet. More than a little scared of what that week might bring. 🫣
