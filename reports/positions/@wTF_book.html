The @wTF Book: UBER & BA

How a whale played two names in public. Their $BA and $UBER books are rebuilt leg by leg from the broker-verified snapshots on their AfterHour posts: every strike, expiry and contract count, what it was worth, and how it changed against the stock. BA ran for 14 months; UBER ran for 6. Both were financed by selling puts, and both were still on when the April 2025 crash hit.

Side by side

Two books, two different games

BA was a 14-month range trade on a falling stock, with big size and big swings. UBER was a shorter trend trade, with smaller legs and a tighter P&L. "Closed legs" uses each leg's last broker mark before it disappeared, not a fill price, and profit taken on partial trims doesn't show up.
