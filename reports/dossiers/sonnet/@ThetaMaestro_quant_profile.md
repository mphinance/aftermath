## 3. Risk Management & PnL Reality

### The documented framework (good)
Post #268 ("My Options Trading Plan," 2025-02-16) is an unusually disciplined, explicit rule set:
- Max **25% of portfolio** per single stock
- 80% of trades expiration **>30 days**; **no 0–7 DTE**
- Hedge 50/50 setups with ITM short-term puts (cost capped at 20% of call value)
- Require a **catalyst** to enter (earnings, macro print, geopolitical event)
- "Never let an option expire worthless — cut early if momentum shifts; let winners run if momentum holds" (Post #35, restated across the archive as his core mantra)

### Where the framework actually worked
- **$WMT**: $70C bought at $3.18, exp. 12/20/24 → sold for **$73K profit (435%)** after two earnings beats (Post #25/#70) — textbook execution of his own rules.
- **$UBER**: his self-described "#1 yolo play," repeated across 6+ posts (Mar–Jun 2025) — entered near a 52-week low, survived an **~80% intraday drawdown**, ultimately closed for **$109K–$130K profit** (figures vary by retelling). This is his flagship "let it ride" story and the one he references most to build credibility.
- **$NVDA LEAPs**: $70K position, down ~90% (bought at $29/contract, "averaged down" to ~$13), recovered to a stated $20K–$75K profit range around the May 2025 earnings print (Post #366).
- **$TTD**: $104K in calls, down 70% intraday, closed a single day later up **$90K** (Post #330).
- **RXRX covered calls**: genuinely smart, underappreciated tactic — sold covered calls against a long RXRX/EXAI stake for **$11,000 in premium income** (Post #262-ish, Feb 2025) while still holding upside. One of the only instances of real options-craft rather than directional gambling.
- **ULTA earnings straddle** (400C/380P, Post #71-72): explicit volatility play, "the only way I lose is if it stays flat" — correctly reasoned, profited on both legs' asymmetry.

### Where the framework broke — and the pattern that matters
1. **Position-size discipline collapsed exactly where it mattered most.** MTPLF grew from 17,000 shares (May 8, 2025) → 41,976 shares/$222K cost basis (Jun 13) → "65,000+ shares" (Oct 18) as **straight equity**, not options — a structural violation of his own 25%-cap, options-first framework, on the single most volatile, least-hedgeable instrument in his history (a Tokyo-listed micro-cap with special-auction trading halts, ¥933 limit-up sessions, and an OTC ADR that gapped ±30% intraday).
2. **He nearly added leverage on top of an already-concentrated bet.** Post #344 (2025-05-15): floats drawing $100K from a **HELOC at a 5.5% promotional rate** to buy $MSTY (a leveraged MSTR-covered-call income ETF) and use the dividend stream to pay down the HELOC. Real-estate-secured debt funding a derivative-income product on top of an already Bitcoin-heavy book is a textbook over-leveraging red flag, regardless of whether it was executed.
3. **He documented the drawdown, then documented nothing else.** Post #464 (2025-08-15, tagged `[Loss][Loss]`): *"I'm down over $100,000... but here's why I'm holding"* — followed by an aggressive, insult-laden defense ("you're either a bad investor or too lazy," "braindead," "stfu and let them cook") aimed at anyone questioning the position. This is a classic capitulation-adjacent top signal: maximal public conviction and hostility toward skeptics, three months before he goes dark.
4. **The fade-to-silence pattern repeats across his career, not just with MTPLF.** RXRX — his single largest 2024 conviction thesis (95 mentions, "10+ a share within a year," "load up boys, we're going to the moon") — simply stops appearing in the archive after February 2025 with **no disclosed exit, no stated profit, no stated loss.** The December 2024 trading contest he organized ($3,000 buy-in, 5 participants, $500 pot) also has **no reported winner or result** anywhere in the archive. He reliably announces convictions and milestones loudly; he does not reliably report their resolution when the resolution is bad.
5. **Terminal state**: Post #485 (2025-11-15, `[Loss][Loss]`) — *"I barely check this app or even my brokerage account... finally logged in today and took a loss porn screenshot... I think my port unlinked from here too. Still holding $MTPLF and $TSWCF."* Tracked portfolio value implied ~$4K (down from a $875K peak four and a half months earlier). Then: near-total silence for 10 months, a "does anyone still use this app" check-in, and a single bitter parting shot in Sep 2026 at a Metaplanet-adjacent commentator ("Kevin Xu ... getting called out for being a POS").

**Net read:** he does not "take profits" as a rule so much as he **takes profits on winners that resolve quickly and holds losers indefinitely while escalating conviction rhetoric**, and this pattern was fatal exactly once — on the position he had sized the largest and hedged the least.
