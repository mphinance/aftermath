"Terridactil" is a **Southern-persona trading educator/influencer** ("Pathfinders" community, co-run with a partner account `@Freeballer`) who posts a blended feed of motivational/lifestyle content, macro-calendar reminders, reactive "look what just happened" market commentary, and occasional thematic small-cap ideas. ## 3. Risk Management & PnL Reality

### 3.1 Does she take profits or hold bags?

Her rhetoric says "take profit into strength" (#9) and "protect your capital" (repeated ~15+ times as a sign-off). Her one detailed win (#9) does show tactical scaling ("added calls higher," "flipped the switch to puts" as the move exhausted) — this is a genuine, granular, believable trade narrative and the single best piece of process evidence in the archive.

However, there is **no bag-holding or stop-loss narrative disclosed at all** — she never describes closing a loser, sizing a stop, or a specific loss on a specific position. The only loss in the archive is an aggregated monthly total with no trade-level detail, which is a **transparency red flag**: winners get play-by-play; the loss gets one paragraph and no forensic breakdown.

Unknown/undisclosed at the trade level. Her own explanation for the -$4,000 month (#118) blames the *environment* — "choppy," "false breakouts," "algorithms running wild," "theta like Pac-Man" — rather than her own entries, sizing, or exits. She does commit afterward to "smaller positions, stronger confirmation, no forcing trades when EMAs/VWAP are tangled" — a reasonable adjustment, but there is no follow-up post in the archive confirming it was applied or that it worked.
