**Does he take profits?** On the wheel: yes, weekly, by design (that is the entire point of theta decay income) — this is his one genuinely well-run process. On directional bets: **no** — the single biggest, most explicit finding in this archive is his own November 2025 confession:

Basically, buying options erased all the gains from selling options and erased some more investments as well... 10 complete wipeouts (-100%)... My selling options strategy kept me afloat, but buying options crushed it."*

**Does he hold bags?** Explicitly, deliberately, and by stated policy: *"Not selling anything and waiting for a turnaround"* (Feb 2025, -48% drawdown); *"cores stay put till they break even"* (Aug 2026, -$249K unrealized). He treats unrealized losses as **not real** until they cross into realized territory — a textbook disposition-effect pattern, applied consistently across 18+ months and two separate account platforms.

**Losing-position management — three consistent patterns:**
1. **Never sells a loser voluntarily.** Every documented large drawdown (Feb–Apr 2025 -69%, Nov 2025 -$173K unrealized, Aug 2026 -$249.6K unrealized) ends in "hold and wait for breakeven," never a stop or a cut.
2. **Cuts winners early out of fear, not discipline.** The UNH YOLO (Aug 2025) was exited a day before an additional ~$200K move — "chickened out," his words. This is the inverse of the bag-holding pattern: fear governs exits on the rare occasions he's actually ahead on a directional bet, while conviction (or inertia) governs the losers.
3. **Stops tracking data he doesn't like.** He explicitly abandoned logging his buying-options/LEAPS P&L ("*I am not tracking the details... it needs a lot of manual work*") starting Nov 2025 — precisely the point that bucket turned decisively negative. The wheel, which stays positive, remains meticulously tracked every single week. **This selective-tracking behavior is itself a quantifiable tell**: when he stops publishing a metric, assume it has turned against him.

**Documented wins.**
- **The wheel itself, mechanically**: $216,547 realized in 2025; $162,008 realized YTD through 2026-09-05 at a $4,591/week run rate — a genuinely repeatable, well-logged, positive-expectancy income process once he stuck to it (Apr–Nov 2025).

**Documented blow-ups.**

- **November 2025 options-buying implosion**: 10 total wipeouts, 14+ losing positions, ~$13K+ in buying-options losses in a single month against a cumulative "$200k+" lifetime admission.
