## 3. Risk Management & PnL Reality

### 3.1 Does he take profits, or hold bags?
**Both, with a clear, stated asymmetry.** Winners get systematically harvested via trailing stops raised as the position climbs (textbook Darvas box behavior): "Sold $PL for 50% profit on a stop loss in profit" (2026-02-04); "Sold NIO for 40% profit today using trailing stop loss" (2025-09-10). Losers, however, are held far longer than winners — he explicitly discloses a **"biggest loser," bag-held for over 2 years** as of 2026-06-10 ("Was hoping to finally exit this year apparently not 🤕. Bag holding it for over 2 years"), and the year-end 2025 posture was "$92,000 gained and $24,000 in unrealized losses" that he was *still deciding* whether to close out for tax-loss harvesting rather than acting immediately (2025-12-10). This is a real, documented disposition-effect pattern: quick to trim winners, slow/reluctant to realize losers, rationalized under a "just holding starter positions, not averaging down" frame.

### 3.2 Self-reported win/loss tagging
Of 875 posts, only 49 are self-tagged "Gain" and 10 "Loss" (≈5:1). This ratio is **not a trading win rate** — it reflects what he chooses to broadcast, and post volume itself became a marketing input after March 2026. Treat all self-labeled outcome posts as survivorship-biased highlight reels, not an audited ledger.

**Contrarian Fade Directives — when to fade him / the retail herd**
1. **Fade the "months since I bought X" hype posts** once a name shows up repeatedly across his and the broader AH follower cohort (ONDS, RKLB, PL are heavily crowded in his own feed and likely across the wider retail-trader universe he draws from) — crowding risk rises with mention frequency.

Treat his "raising cash" posts as a mild contrarian bullish tell on the broader tape, not a risk-off signal to copy.
3. **Discount any "3-year journey" or aggregate net-worth milestone post by default** — these are the least verifiable, most promotionally-motivated numbers in the corpus and should never move a model's confidence score.

**Mandatory Risk Blacklists & Firewalls**
1. **Do not inherit his income/yield-product exposure uncritically** (MSTY, ULTY, similar synthetic-covered-call income ETFs) — his own data shows NAV erosion consistently offsetting the advertised distribution yield; flag these as a NAV-decay firewall category regardless of who is buying them.

**Exit Rules & Alpha Rectification**
1. Mirror his **trailing-stop-after-profit mechanic** (raise stop once position is +30–40%, not before) as a generic exit-discipline template — it is his best-evidenced, most consistently applied rule.

3. **Rebalance sizing away from his "feeler $500–$1,000" convention** where account size differs materially — the sizing rule is sound in spirit (small initial commitment, scale into confirmed winners) but his absolute dollar anchors are personal, not systematic, and should be converted to a % of NAV before use.
